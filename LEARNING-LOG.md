# Learning log

The record of using your tutor for real, as a learner and not as its builder.
The bench tells you how `final` does on fixed tests; this file tells you how it did when you actually tried to learn something with it.

Hold at least three real learning sessions with `final`, on one of your two test topics.
Start each one in a terminal in `tutor/` with `claude`, and ask for whatever you need as a learner: a lesson, a quiz, grading, or a conversation.
Fill in a row of the sessions table right after each session, while you still remember what it felt like.

Your repo is public, and so are the learner files your sessions leave in `tutor/`, so keep personal details out of this log and out of them.

Each section ends with a marker line.
Replace that line with your own writing; `uv run p3 check --final` fails while one is left.
Please write this yourself: it is about your experience, and the agent does not have it.
